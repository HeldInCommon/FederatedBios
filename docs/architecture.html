Held in Common.

Your work lives on a machine you control — and travels only as far as you say.

Why.
Why we built this

You made the work.
You should keep the say.

Creative work is being swept up to train AI — usually with no credit, no consent, no way to say no. Held in Common keeps it on your own machine and lets you attach the terms it travels under.

What.
Two words you'll hear all day

A node.

The app running on your own computer. It holds your profile and your work. Nobody sees what's on it unless you send them a copy. Think of it as your studio.

Two words you'll hear all day

A commons.

A shared directory others can browse. You send a copy of your profile to it — or you don't. Anyone can run one: a studio, a class, a festival, another artist. A noticeboard the community keeps together.

The simplest shape

One shared
commons.

Everyone runs a node, then sends a copy to one directory. Easy to search — one place to find each other.
