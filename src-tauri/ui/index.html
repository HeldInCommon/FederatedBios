Held in Common · first launch

    
What should we call this node?

    
People see this name next to your profile on every commons you share to. If you run Held in Common on more than one computer, give each its own name, like “Studio laptop” or “Home desktop”.

    
Earlier versions used this computer's account name. From now on they'll use the name you choose here.

    

    

      
      
      

        
        
      

    

    

  


  
  

    
Held in Common · local commons

    
Remove the local commons?

    
Everything shared to it, approved on it or made on it is deleted, and people on this Wi-Fi can no longer reach it. Your node and your online commons stay exactly as they are. You can open a new local commons from the Go menu any time.

    

      
      
    

    

  


  
  

    
Held in Common · open to the room

    
Open your node to the room?

    
Anyone on this Wi-Fi will be able to see your public profile and public stories. Signing in, your keys and anything members-only stay on this computer.

    
Only open this on a network you trust: a classroom, a studio, your own hotspot. It closes when you choose Close in the Go menu, when you quit the app, and when this computer's network address changes (the app checks every minute).
